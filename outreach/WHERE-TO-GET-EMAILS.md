# Where to get REAL working emails (companies that are running)

Do **not** invent `founder@` / `firstname.lastname@` addresses. Guessed emails bounce and burn your domain.

## Best sources (exact emails)

| Tool | What you get | Notes |
|------|----------------|-------|
| [Hunter.io](https://hunter.io) Domain Search | Public emails on a company domain | Free credits/day — start here |
| [Apollo.io](https://apollo.io) | Founder / CTO / Eng Manager emails | Filter: SaaS, 1–50 employees |
| [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com) → Sales Nav / free | Name + company → then Hunter verify | Best for ICP targeting |
| Company website Contact / Impressum | `hello@` `hi@` `support@` | Only if published |
| [NeverBounce](https://neverbounce.com) / [ZeroBounce](https://zerobounce.net) | Valid / invalid / catch-all | Verify before send |

## Daily workflow (15–40 verified)

1. Apollo/LinkedIn: find **Founder or CTO** at SaaS, **1–50 employees**, US/CA/UK.
2. Copy their **domain** (e.g. `acme.com`).
3. Hunter → Domain Search → pick published email (prefer personal over `info@` when available).
4. NeverBounce/ZeroBounce → only send if **Valid** (skip Catch-all if you want safer).
5. Paste into Day-1 sheet below → send from `info@testsynclab.com`.
6. Cap: **15/day** first week (then ramp). 40/day only after domain warms + tool sending.

## Filters that mean “company is running”

- Website loads, product exists, pricing or login/signup live
- LinkedIn company page active (recent posts / headcount)
- Not agency/consulting farm; they ship their own product
- Prefer companies that raised recently OR are hiring engineers (shipping)

## Do not email

- Fabricated Gmail/Yahoo personal addresses
- `noreply@` / `donotreply@`
- Giant enterprises from the old CSV contact-forms (Linear/Vercel sales forms) as “cold email”
